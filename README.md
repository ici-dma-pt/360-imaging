# 360 Imaging

A 360 image is a special kind of picture that lets you look all around — not just in front of you, but behind, above, and below too! It’s like standing inside the photo. When you move your head or swipe the screen, the picture moves with you. It feels like you're really there, inside a room, a forest, or even outer space!

![](assets/images/1/sky-ground.png)

To make a 360 image, you need a camera that can see in every direction at once, or a special computer program that stitches lots of photos together. It takes all the views — left, right, up, and down — and wraps them into a big ball shape. Then, when you look at it with your phone or a VR headset, it feels like you’re inside the scene, exploring with your eyes.
